# Code of conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. The full text is at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ and applies here as if it were written out in this file. The Contributor Covenant is by the Contributor Covenant organisation and is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, accept feedback on your work gracefully, and do not harass or demean anyone. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews or any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to `CONDUCT_CONTACT_PLACEHOLDER`. Reports are handled in confidence, and the maintainers follow the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the behaviour.
